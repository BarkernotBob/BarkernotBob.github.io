# TOC

1. If you aspire to the great goal of obedience to the law, you must first learn of the effectual means to attain that end.
2. To begin, you must receive 4 provisions from God
    1. A heart inclined towards obedience to the law.
    2. A persuasion that you are reconciled to God.
    3. A persuasion that you will enjoy everlasting happiness with God.
    4. A persuasion that God will provide you with enough strength to desire, and enough strength to perform all that His law requires.
3. The way get these provisions is to receive them from Christ's fullness by union and fellowship with Him.
4. The means of all fellowship and union with Christ are the gospel and faith.
5. Without this union, it is impossible to attain any holiness through the actions of our natural state
Direction I. To **perform** the duties **required** in the law, **first learn** the **effectual means** to attain **so great an end**

Direct. II. Four **<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">endowments</mark> and qualifications necessary** (presumably necessary to perform the duties of the law); 1. An **inclination and propensity of heart** thereunto. 2. A **persuasion of our reconciliation** with God. 3. A **persuasion of our enjoyment of everlasting happiness**. 4. A **persuasion of sufficient strength both to will and perform** duties acceptably

Direct. III. The way to get these <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">endowments</mark>, to enable for practice, is, to **receive them from Christ**’s fulness, by **union and fellowship** with him

Direct. IV. The **means or instruments of this union**, and **all fellowship**, are, **the gospel and faith**. What faith is

Direct. V. We **cannot attain holiness by our endeavours** in a natural state, **without union** and fellowship **with Christ**

Direct. VI. Those that **endeavour sincere obedience, as the condition to procure** a right and title to **salvation**, and as a ground to trust on Christ, **do seek salvation by the works of the law**

Direct. VII. We are **not to imagine**, that our hearts and lives must be **changed, from sin to holiness, in any measure, before we trust on Christ**

Direct. VIII. **Seek for holiness** only in its due order, **after union, justification, and the gift of the Holy Ghost; and by faith**

Direct. IX. We must **first** receive the **comforts of the gospel**, that we may **perform the duties of the law**

Direct. X. That we may, by gospel comforts, **perform the duties of the law,** we **must get assurance** in that very faith whereby we receive Christ

Direction XI. **Believe on Christ**, in a right manner, **without delay**, and then continue and **increase in faith**; **that enjoyment of Christ, and union and fellowship with him, and all holiness by him, may be begun, continued, and increased**

Direct. XII. **Diligently use faith for the performance of the duties of the law**, by walking no longer according to your old state principles, or means of practice; but only according to that new state you receive by faith, and its principles, and means of practice

Direct. XIII. Make a **right use of all the means appointed in the word**, for **obtaining and practicing holiness**, only in this way of believing and walking in Christ, according to your new state

Direct. XIV. That you may thus seek holiness only by believing and walking in Christ, **take encouragement from the great advantages of this way, and the excellent properties of it**
