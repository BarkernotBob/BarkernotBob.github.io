# Application Monitoring in Teams
* **App Monitoring** is just to be used for announcements when an application is down or back up; no conversations about troubleshooting.
* **Incident Response** is for further troubleshooting if an app doesn't have a dedicated teams channel.
* If an app has a **dedicated teams channel**, troubleshooting should happen in the app's **IT Support** group, with highly technical discussions housed in the **IT Staff** channel.
