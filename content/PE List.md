1. **Validation Issues (Especially Multi-Level)** - Excessive or inconsistent validations litter ProducerEngage and are causing confusion.
2. **Navigation Blocked by Errors** - Agents cannot move between screens when an error is present unless they use the save-and-close workaround. This is one of the most frequent complaints and is slowing down quoting significantly. Most agents are having to "save and close" 2-3 times per interaction with ProducerEngage.
3. **"System Bouncing"**- This is currently one of the most common and frustrating complaints. There are some workflows that require users to go in and out of ProducerEngage and Account Center to complete one task. For example, if I want to discover which form is on a policy and view it, I need to go from Account Center to ProducerEngage to see which form is on the policy, then all the way back out to AC and manually search for the form to view. 



**BUGS**
1. **Overall Slowness in PE** - Performance degradation is especially noticeable when moving from Account Center into ProducerEngage, editing a quote, and when rating. The system is perceptibly no better than AC. There has been no meaningful improvement in quoting or rating speed from the user perspective, and this remains a top complaint.
2. **After-Hours Performance Problems** - We don't have a ton of details about this, due to randomness, but system performance seems to drop significantly after hours, further contributing to delays and abandoned work. The call we receive in the morning is typically "I could hardly work last night and now it seems to be okay."
3. **Unexpected Logouts and Timeout errors** - This is the 400 error from earlier this week. Users are being logged out of ProducerEngage, and the system forces AC access for re-entry. This disrupts workflow and is a major pain point. The fix only made the error look better, and there are still disruptions.
4.  **PolicyCenter-Only Issues Surfacing in PE** - *Problems that originate in PC are presenting inside PE, creating issues that agents cannot resolve and support cannot easily trace. For example, specific validations that cannot be worked around in PE require a call to us to fix in PolicyCenter.*
