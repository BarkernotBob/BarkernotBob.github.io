# **What makes a Church Father?**
* Really old
* *Usually* they were godly men
* *usually* They held to orthodoxy
* Historical church recognition 

# **Phases of the Fathers?**
**Early Phase**: Everyone spoke and wrote greek. The Greek writing went from Koine to Byzantine within this time period
**Later Phase**: Around the 4th century, where Latin starts to dominate

# Why do the Fathers still matter?
* As humans, living in Christ in the age of the Church, seeking to understand his word and be obedient to him, **the things that mattered to them should continue to matter to us.**
* [09:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=579&v=uU2ar2nXFLY) Their context was similar to ours **in the fact that Paganism and Pluralism was the cultural context they lived in**
* We can **learn from their mistakes**, and they may have **clearer eyes to diagnose our mistakes**
* They help us understand how we got where we are today.
* They provide explanation and defense for doctrinal disputes that get recycled in the modern era. 

# Literal or Allegorical Interpretation?
* **Antiochian** hermeneutic was more *literal*
* **Alexandrian** hermeneutic was more *allegorical*. He argues this was a response to culture, and an attempt to reach their culture effectively.
