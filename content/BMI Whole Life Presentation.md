# Long Term Care
* Must lose 2 ADL's
* LTC often applies in old age, major injury, cognitive impairment, or chronic illness.
* LTC is no longer available as a stand-alone policy. 
* ~ 70% over 65 need
* 40% of claims are for 18-64
* $100k a year for nursing home
* ~ $33k a year for in-home care, 5 days a week
* Medicare covers some, but not most costs related to LTC. 
* Medicaid only covers if you've exhausted nearly all of your assets, and you're put in a state funded facility. 
* Lifetime cost of care for someone with dementia is $321k. 

# Mass Mutual
* **Advances (not in addition to)** your life insurance coverage, up to 75% of the total.
    * Has an 18% fee when used.
* Is lifetime coverage; doesn't end when you terminate employment. 
* Covers you, spouse, kids, grandkids
* **Is Dividend eligible**
* Spouses must be under 60, kids under 26 years.
* Opens May 11th
* https://totalis.foleon.com/brotherhood/mass-mutual/
