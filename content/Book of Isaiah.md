## Background
* Written between 740 and 690
    * Isaiah as the single author is the best biblical interpretation. Many quibble with him directly naming Cyrus in 44-45, but this is based on an assumption that he couldn't have known prophetically the name of this one to come so far into the future. The NT attributes both the front and back half of the book to Isaiah many many times.
* Mainly focused towards Judah, the Southern Kingdom. This contains Jerusalem.
* Written after the fall of the Northern Kingdom of Israel
