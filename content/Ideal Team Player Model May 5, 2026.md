# Humble
Quick to point out the contributions of others, slow to seek attention for their own. Share credit, emphasize team over self, and define success collectively.
* Was Jesus Humble?
* Have you ever thought about someone who lacks self-confidence as lacking humility?
* How do you simultaneously foster security and humility?
**Outcomes:**
* Vulnerable, builds trust, honest in conflict, and holds others accountable. 
# Hungry
A manageable and sustainable commitment to doing a job well and going above and beyond when it is truly required. 
* Hunger must be appropriately directed
    * Being more hungry about your career than honoring your values
    * Being more hungry about your hobbies than your work
    * Being hungry towards your advancement at the expense of others
* So Hunger is the drive, the motivation. But it must be aligned with our values or else it actually is a negative. 
* Hungry does not mean you're never satisfied with your pay, your position, or your role as if it required a lack of contentment. But it does mean you will always be looking for new ways to be as helpful as you can be. 
**Outcomes**
* Engages in uncomfortable conflict when necessary, holds others accountable, and takes the difficult path when it's necessary to achieve results.
# Smart
Smart people have good judgment and intuition around the subtleties of group dynamics and the impact of their words and actions. 

**Outcomes**
* Avoids unnecessary interpersonal problems, tactful engagement with others, and able to hold others accountable.  


**Humility** is the *value*, **Hunger** is the *drive*, and **Smart** is the *strategy*
