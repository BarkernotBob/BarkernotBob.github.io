Friction is sometimes good. 
* The easier money is to access, the more likely you are to use it
* The easier it is to get away with a crime without anyone knowing, the more likely it will happen
* Learning can be more effective if the information is challenging us
* If snacks are up the road instead of in the cabinet, I'm less likely to overeat them
