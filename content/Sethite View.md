**Sethite View - Summary of Interpretive positions**

1. The “Sons of God” in Genesis 6 is referring to humans in the common NT sense of **Christians** and not in the OT sense of **Spiritual Beings** shown in Job.
2. The descriptors of the offspring of the “Daughters of Man” and the “Sons of God” is describing something like “they became fierce warriors.” There is no need to say that “Nephilim,” “Mighty men,” and “Men of Renown” are supernatural descriptions.
3. The later reference to the Nephilim in Numbers 13 is also a description of natural men who have come to be so fierce and large as to be called even giants (such as Goliath).
4. The flood narrative in the book of 1 Enoch is wrong and does not reflect the reality of the events in any meaningful way.
5. Jude and Peter are either not referencing 1 Enoch, or at least not in a way that would show they support the book’s narrative of the flood.
6. The Septuagint translators of the Bible were wrong when they translated “Sons of God” in Genesis 6:2 as “Angels of God.”
7. The Septuagint translators of the Bible were wrong when they translated “Nephilim” and “Mighty Men” as “Giants.”
8. The (So far as we can tell historically) universal pre-christian Jewish interpretation that these “Sons of God” were Spiritual Beings was wrong.
9. The (So far as we can tell historically) universal early church interpretation for ~ 300 years that these “Sons of God” were Spiritual Beings was wrong.
