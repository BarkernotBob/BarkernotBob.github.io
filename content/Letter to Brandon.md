Hey Brother. I was hoping to get to quickly share this with you at your wedding, but since I didn't get the chance now you get the longer version in writing. 


> [!NOTE] BIG IDEA
> The Central Christian Ethic is not Strength
> It is also not Weakness
> It is dependence on God for his strength, and mercy from Him because we sin
> So the Christian can be quick to acknowledge weakness and our creaturely estate
> For when we are weak, then (and only then) we are strong
