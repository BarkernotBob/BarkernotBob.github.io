DT 32, 

1 COR 10

Clearly, The Rock is the central name or title of God which is communicated in this song and is contrasted with how other gods hold up as a rock. It is interesting that this song, one of the last words from Moses, is so focused in on this one title for God. This is also the word that Paul uses to describe Christ in 1 Cor 10:4
