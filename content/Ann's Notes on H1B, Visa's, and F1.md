Immigration Process

- Visa Types
    - F1 - right out of school giving them a few years work authorization
        - BH does not have to pay for anything
        - When expires, apply for H1B
    - H1B - Bigger impact at BH as there are significant expenses an employer incurs
        - Have to have an employer that will sponsor for H1B
        - We don't have to sponsor it or hire the person if they need an H1B
        - How do we find out? We can find out before offer
            - Question is asked on the application
            - Are we willing to sponsor someone for this posting, so best process would be to indicate in job posting we are not willing to sponsor someone in this job if we don't want to sponsor a person (made decision due to H1B status)
                - Fees come out of manager's budget
            - An individual cannot sponsor themselves for H1B
- Associated Costs
    - H1B not guaranteed
    - Can be a massive boost to recruiting process if willing to do H1B as many companies are not willing to sponsor
    - Applying for H1B lottery (65,000 granted each year in entire USA and way more apply) is $1,000
        - Lottery can be entered 3 times and if not selected and if F1 visa runs out, they can no longer work in US and they have to be terminated
            - F1 visas can be extended when obtaining a master's degree in a Stem field
            - There can be multiple master degrees
        - Selected, then have another $4K to $5K to process
        - Employee cannot contribute to this
        - If Employee not selected in lottery, we lost the $1K
            - Can't make an employee pay back any of the money
        - They can resign the day their H1B is approved
        - Entering lottery only takes a few days to prep lottery application (Mar 6 - 22nd 2024)
            - 2 weeks for results to come out
            - Have 90 days to submit application
            - Standard processing of H1B can take months
                - While waiting for approval can have issues with other things like driver's license renewal
            - Premium processing (employees can bear this cost) and it is $2500 - BH will typically not cover
                - Benefit is H1B approved in a few weeks
        - How long does H1B last - initial one is 3 yrs and can be extended an additional 3 years
        - If they don't get a green card, then they are done and can no longer work in the USA or enter the H1B lottery again
            - Can their job title change during the H1B process?
            - To qualify for H1B, their bachelor's have to be in a specialized field
        - We cannot discriminate based on national origin, their accent or color of their skin
        - New employer would have to incur the fees for transfer of H1B
    - Green Card
        - Next step after H1B
        - Lawful permanent resident
        - Can't discriminate against someone who has one
        - They are allowed to be here
