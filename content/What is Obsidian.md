* Friction is your friend...Sometimes. Because sometimes, this friction can act as
    1. A filter. Keeping you from adding crap you don't need.
    2. Forces you to think critically about what you're working on. See [Veritasium, "The Illusion of Truth"](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=cebFWOlx848) In this video, near the end, he talks about how a test written in a false syllogism was more likely to be answered correctly *if* the text was written out in a hard to read font. His explanation is that this is because your brain is not allowed to be so comfortable approaching the question, which forces you to think critically, which allows you to see the error in the syllogism. 
- Don't make a personal Wiki. 
    1. Sometimes you can leave old info wrong or unedited. Have a conversation with yourself when you disagree or learn more. 
    2. Write to stimulate thinking and learning. Don't write simply copy/paste or transcribe things you've heard
