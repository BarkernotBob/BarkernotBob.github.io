**Filing is State Regulated**
* Operating committee decides when we need a new filing
* Scope
    * LOB
    * rate/rules
    * form additions/changes
    * dec or app updates
* Legal takes care of the changes for all added and updated forms
    * Often the same change gets added to many forms due to state specific forms
    * Both claims and UW have to sign off on these changes
* Forms sent to R&D
    * New and old forms are compared
