Chiasm is related to an “x” or the idea of crossing

“Chiasm highlights central themes, clarifies meaning through parallel elements, and reveals the overall purpose of a passage or book. Ignoring chiastic structures limits understanding of the text’s artistry and intended message.”

[**The Value of Chiasm for New Testament Interpretation**](https://ref.ly/res/LLS:gs_bsac_141/2015-10-27T16:51:03Z/401928)

Ronald E. Man (1984)

“The purpose of this device is to emphasize relationships between elements, whether synonymous or antithetical”

“While this pattern occurs in Hebrew literature from the verse level to the book level, some biblical readers see chiasms all over the place when they are not there. That admitted, Daniel 2 through 7 does appear to be in a chiastic relationship.”

Longman, Tremper, III. [_How to Read Daniel_](https://ref.ly/logosres/howtoreaddaniel?ref=Bible.Da&off=23972&ctx=otentially+further.%0a~While+this+pattern+o). IVP Academic: An Imprint of InterVarsity Press, 2020, p. 14.
