# Miscellany
* Isaiah 5:8-10 seems to indicate a dual indictment to accumulation, that in the first place there is nothing leftover for others, and on the other hand you've separated yourself from everyone else. This is physically true when thinking about someone buying up a bunch of land they don't need (it becomes harder for poorer people to buy land, and they have so much land that they don't have neighbors for miles in each direction) but it seems this is also true on the principal of it. If you're accumulating simply for yourself, it will be to the detriment of others and yourself. You will be alone. You will never get the yield you hoped for.
