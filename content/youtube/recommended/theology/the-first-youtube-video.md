### How to add your own (delete this section when you're done)

1. Pick the right section folder inside **`content/youtube`** — `subscriptions`, `recommended`, `chapel-messages`, or `my-content` — then copy this file into it and rename it (e.g. `my-favorite-talk.md`).
2. Change the `title:` at the top.
3. On YouTube, open the video → **Share** → copy the link (any YouTube link works — `watch?v=…`, a `youtu.be/…` short link, even with the extra `?si=…` tracking bits).
4. Paste the link into an embed line like this — just put your link between the `![](` and `)`:

   `![](PASTE_YOUR_LINK_HERE)`

   So a finished line looks like: `![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jNQXAC9IVRw)`
5. Write your note about it underneath. Then **Publish**.

Prefer just a link instead of an inline player? Drop the embed and write a normal link to the video.
